                                                                               While this classical mechanical description effectively captures
                                                                            crack initiation and separation processes, it does not account for
                                                                            additional thermodynamic contributions--such as heat generation
                                                                            or irreversible entropy production--that may arise at highly stressed
                                                                            or sliding interfaces. In addition, classical CZM formulations
                                                                            typically trigger damage only when the relative displacement
                                                                            reaches a critical initiation value. This displacement-based criterion
                                                                            does not represent subcritical cyclic sliding or the continuous
                                                                            entropy generation required by the second law of thermodynamics.
                                                                            As highlighted in recent advances such as the unified mechanics
                                                                            theory [32], any deformation--regardless of its magnitude--
                                                                            produces irreversible entropy and thus contributes to progressive
                                                                            material degradation. The present work adopts the conventional
                                                                            displacement-based CZM formulation due to its widespread use and
                                                                            practicality in micro-electronic interface modeling, while acknowl
                                                                            edging that thermodynamic-based damage descriptions fall outside
                                                                            the scope of this study but represents an important direction for
                                                                            future extension.
