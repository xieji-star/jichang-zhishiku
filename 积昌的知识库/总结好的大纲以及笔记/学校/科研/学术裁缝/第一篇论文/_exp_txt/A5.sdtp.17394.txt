1. Introduction                                                               Figure 2. Stacked structure of a flexible OLED panel.
   Flexible OLED panels consist of multiple layers of films, and

each film is laminated with Optically Clear Adhesive (OCA).
Colorless Polyimide (CPI) film has good properties of bending
resistance, high light transmission and conductivity. CPI film in a
flexible OLED panel can reduce the overall thickness of a panel
and improve the optical performance and flexibility. However,
CPI film is most prone to plastic deformation in a flexible panel,
which can affect the display reliability and service life of flexible
panels. Therefore, how to reduce the plastic deformation of CPI
film becomes an important issue in designing the stacking
structure of flexible OLED panels.

   Pengle Dang studied the structural reliability of flexible OLED
panels, and the results showed that the stress gradually increases
with the decrease of bending radius, and the stress situation
becomes more complicated with the decrease of bending radius
[1]. Zhengzhou Liu conducted a specific stress and strain analysis
of the bending process of flexible OLED panels, and investigated
the effects of different stacking structures and backplane stiffness
on the panel stress [2]. Niu et al. investigated the effect of bending
mode on stress distribution during panel folding, and the
experimental results showed that the water-drop-shaped bending
can effectively improve the structural stability of flexible panels
and reduce the risk of panel damage [3].

   In this paper, the finite element simulation method is used to
establish the simulation model of flexible OLED panel containing
CPI film. The simulation analysis of the flexible module focuses
on the strain of the CPI film and the OLED in the flexible module.
By adjusting the water-drop-shaped bending structure of the
flexible module, the stacking order of the flexible module, and the
thickness of each OCA layer in the flexible module, the bending

can be used in combination with a backplane and located below
the OLED. CPI film can also be used on its own and laminated on
top of the OLED. Plastic deformation of CPI film can be reduced
by adjusting the stacked structure of the flexible module.
