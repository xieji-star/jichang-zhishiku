In order to ensure the foldability of the foldable screen, it has to
maintain a low fold stress through optimization of the foldable
module stackup and materials. The stress and strain distribution
generated in each layer can be analyzed using finite element
method [1]. In recent years, this lead to the development of
various simulation models to predict the reliability of
multilayered structures, to gain structural understanding, and to
improve design.

Existing simulation only focused on analyzing the stress of
foldable module in the folded state [2,3]. Based on these analysis,
the adhesive failure that occurs in the outward folding area of the
water-drop shape structure can be well understood. Adhesive
failure is usually related to micro defects such as cavities [4]. The
hydrostatic tension stress plays an important driving role in the
growth of voids, therefore, the debonding index based on pressure

the display is opened within 1 second. In case C, the module was
