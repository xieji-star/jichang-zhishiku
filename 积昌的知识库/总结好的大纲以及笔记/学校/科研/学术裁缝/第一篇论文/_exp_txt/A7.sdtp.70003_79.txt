2.1 WettabilityAttachment modulusand ball dropping ability

      The previous text has already explained the relationship
between modulus and wettability through JKL contact theory. Now,
we are developing Acrylic UV PSA adhesives with different moduli
to characterize the relationship between wettability and product anti
ball ability; The Tg point of this adhesive system can be designed
to be below -20 . Prior to UV, the internal cross-linking degree
of the colloid is low, the movement space of the chain segments is
large, the modulus at room temperature is  50Kpa, and the
softness of the colloid is high. After UV, secondary cross-linking is
carried out by exciting free groups and doping reactants, and the
cross-linking density is increased. The modulus at room
temperature can be increased to the range of 100-500Kpa, and the
colloid hardness is improved.
