bending radius for an inorganic TFT without any
mechanical damage to inorganic layers, which causes
electrical degradation on TFTs. For example, critical
bending radii of 7 mm and 9 mm were reported for
SiOxNy and ITO, respectively, on 125 m and 180 m
thick PET plastic substrates.9­11 Someya et al. also
reported the bending properties on organic thin film tran-
sistors (OTFT) on 125 m thick PEN plastic substrate.12
Recently several companies have reported the develop-
ment of foldable display which is robust up to 4 mm of
bending radius.3,4 In this paper, mechanical and electri-
cal properties of low-temperature polycrystalline silicon
(LTPS) thin film transistors (TFTs) were evaluated. Based
on the simulation result of the stress distribution in TFT
backplane, several new approaches were suggested for
strain-release of thin-film electron devices on flexible
substrate. The new panel structure was able to be robust
at a small bending radius of 1 mm, thereby enabling
manufacture of foldable and rollable displays. Finally, it
was suggested that organic dielectric material could tre-
mendously reduce the stress exerted on TFT.
